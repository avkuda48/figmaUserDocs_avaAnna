# Troubleshooting

In this article, you will find solutions to common issues you may run into while following this documentation.

## Crashes or Application Errors

If your Figma app is crashing, not saving your work automatically, or giving any other application related error, here are some common problems and their solutions.

### Failed Saves or Application Crashes

The Figma web app requires an internet connection to run and save your work. Please ensure that your device is connected to the internet. VPNs or certain browser extensions might interfere with your connection and prevent Figma from accessing the internet.

### Application Running Poorly

Figma requires a decent amount of ram to run quickly and smoothly. Check that you do not have any other tabs open, especially additional Figma tabs. After reaching 60%, Figma displays a banner depicting how much browser ram is in use. If it reaches 100%, the application will not be able to run. [Check out this page](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040528173) for information on how to reduce your ram usage.

If issues persist, try closing the tab and starting your browser again. If you continue to run into problems, consider [reporting the bug](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041468234) to Figma.

## Adding Collaborators

If you have correctly added your collaborators and they still cannot see or access your Figma project, here are some common solutions.

### Collaborator’s Email has no Associated Figma Account

All collaborators must have an email with a registered Figma account before they are able to see and edit files in your project.
Wrong Permissions
Check that you have given your collaborator the correct permissions. [Read this article](task1.md) to see how to change a collaborator’s permissions.

## Image Resolution

Sometimes, imported images and videos can appear pixelated or low resolution. Some common reasons for this and how to fix it are listed below.

### Poor Network Connection or Low Ram

If your network connection is weak or you are using a lot of browser ram, Figma will automatically lower the resolution of your images and videos for better performance. When you export your file, the content will be at its proper resolution.

### Incorrect Frame Size

If your frame size is very large or very small compared to your image or video, it can affect the resolution quality especially after exporting. Ensure that the pixel count of your imported content aligns with the size of your frame in Figma.

## Prototyping

Prototyping can be very complicated, but there are a few common issues with simple solutions.

### Prototype Displays too Large or Small in Present Mode

If your design appears way zoomed in or out in present mode, it’s most likely that your frame size is incorrect. Present mode will display content true to size, so be sure that your frame is the correct ratio and pixel count.

### Buttons or Menu Options do not Lead to Designated Page

If your buttons or menu icons are clickable but do not take the user to the connected frame, chances are that your action or destination is incorrect. In the prototype settings, click on the blue arrow on your actionable item and check that the trigger, action, and destination settings are correct. If they are not, click on the option’s dropdown menu and select the correct option.

If you encounter any other issues not listed here, check out the [Figma Troubleshooting Guide](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040523973-Troubleshooting-checklist) for solutions.
